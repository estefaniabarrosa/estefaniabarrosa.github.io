A little about me

From kitchens to data, with the same curiosity

I started in hospitality. I studied Hotel Administration in Caracas and spent years in kitchens and hotels, where I learned that good service depends on planning, details and knowing your customer.

In Barcelona I helped run an artisan gelato shop. I forecast demand for every flavour, costed every recipe and built the sales tracking we used to decide what to make. That season, sales grew 65%. That's when I understood that numbers can be a very human tool.

Since 2023 I live in Lisbon. I work in operations and quality for international platforms, and as acting supervisor I helped a team of 15 go from about 60% to 85% quality. On the side I help small brands read their marketing results and decide where to put their energy.

This year I added the technical side with SQL, Python and Tableau, so now I can go from the raw data to the decision on my own.
